About SmartMandi

Farmers need to compare market prices, transportation expenses and crop spoilage before choosing where to sell their produce. SmartMandi estimates the net return from different markets.

Calculate Your Expected Profit

How It Works

1. Enter the crop and quantity.

2. Estimate how much crop may spoil.

3. Compare the estimated returns after transport costs.

4. Review the market with the highest estimated return.
