About SmartMandi

Farmers need to compare market prices, transportation expenses and crop spoilage before choosing where to sell their produce. SmartMandi helps farmers estimate returns and compare sample markets.

How It Works

1. Select a crop and enter the available quantity.

2. Estimate the percentage of crop that may spoil.

3. Compare sample prices and transportation costs.

4. Review the market with the highest estimated net return.
